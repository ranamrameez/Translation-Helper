AL Habib fee and break-even guide

Intraday (same-day square-off): brokerage on one leg, plus estimated other charges of 0.03–0.04%. The total is approximately 0.18–0.19% at PKR 20 and above.

Multi-day: brokerage on both legs, plus estimated other charges of 0.06–0.10% combined. The total is approximately 0.36–0.40% at PKR 20 and above.

Brokerage per charged leg is the greater of PKR 0.03 per share and 0.15%. Move estimates use the entry price, round up to PKR 0.01, and exclude capital gains tax and slippage.
